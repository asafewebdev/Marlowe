Description

A faux fur coat that does the work of an outfit. The oversized collar and defined waist do most of the talking. You just wear it.

  • Oversized volume collar that frames the face and keeps the wind out
  • Self fabric belt to draw in the waist, tied loose or fastened at the front
  • Dropped shoulder and relaxed sleeve for an easy fit over knitwear
  • Twin front pockets, deep enough for gloves
  • Concealed hook and eye closures beneath the belt for a clean front
  • Fully lined in a satin touch fabric that slips on over anything

Wear it over black for the evening, or belted over jeans and a roll neck by day. The Marlowe is the coat you'll reach for all winter.

Model wears a UK size M and is 5'9" (175cm). The fit is relaxed through the body, so size down for a closer cut.

Not another viral coat

You've seen the ones that arrive flat, shed everywhere and itch. This isn't that. Densely piled faux fur that holds its shape and doesn't shed, a satin touch lining that slips over knitwear, and a belt with proper body to it. Picked and posted from our London warehouse.

Product Details & Care

Composition & Fit

  • Shell: 100% modacrylic faux fur
  • Lining: 100% polyester
  • Belt: self-fabric tie belt, fully removable
  • Relaxed, oversized fit, true to size
  • Style reference: MW-2201-COAT

Care Instructions

  • Specialist dry clean only
  • Do not tumble dry or iron directly on the fur
  • Store on a padded hanger, away from direct sunlight
  • Brush gently with a wide-tooth comb to restore pile
  • Keep away from open flame and direct heat sources
